# Ledger Takeoff and consolidated purchasing

- Added structural Ledger lumber to Framing Takeoff, separate from Ledger fasteners.
- Ledger stock inherits the dominant Joist Field size in its Deck Boundary and defaults to 2×6 PT.
- Planned Ledger runs into commercial lumber lengths while retaining their originating edge identities.
- Added a Detailed by use Takeoff format for construction review and editing.
- Added a read-only Consolidated for purchase format that groups identical stock across Ledger, Joist, Blocking, Rim / Flush, and Beam roles.
- Added detailed or consolidated PDF/quote and JSON export behavior without changing authoritative model geometry.
