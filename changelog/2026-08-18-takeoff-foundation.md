# Editable Takeoff foundation

Date: 2026-08-18

Status: Implemented

CME now converts the current construction model into an editable material takeoff available under **Export options**. Expandable categories support automatic, adjusted, and manual material lines. Initial automatic recipes cover grooved field decking, square-edge picture frame, fascia, stair covering, and Wild Hog railing components.

Calculated quantities remain visible when the user applies an override. Unit prices are optional, manual materials persist with the project, and automatic overrides can be reset to the latest model calculation.

The Takeoff workspace can produce a supplier-facing PDF without prices, an internal priced PDF, and structured JSON for future DCR Sales Hub connectivity.
