# Wild Hog handrail stock optimization

## Implemented

- `2×6 DW handrail` is calculated independently for every straight railing run.
- Consecutive panels are grouped under continuous commercial boards with any joints located at railing posts.
- CME selects 8, 10, 12, 16, or 20-foot stock by minimum purchased footage and then minimum piece count.
- Example: two panels totaling 9′ 4″ now produce one 2×6×10 instead of two 2×6×8 pieces.
